Executive Summary of Findings

The claim that generative AI models will replace all software engineers within three years is unsupported by empirical computer science literature and contradicting official workforce projections. While autonomous coding agents dramatically accelerate routine syntax boilerplate and unit test generation, current models exhibit severe degradation when managing distributed architectures, security invariants, and real-world system debugging. Independent research from ACM and IEEE confirms human oversight remains mandatory, and national labor bodies forecast sustained growth in software engineering employment over the next decade.
