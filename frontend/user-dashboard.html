TruthLens Citizen Contributor Standards

1. Link Primary Media

Always supply the original post URL, archive link, or unedited video timestamp whenever possible.

2. Neutral Verbatim

State the asserted rumor exactly as claimed without editorial spin or inflammatory framing.

3. Synthetic Media Flag

If a rumor appears generated by AI diffusion or voice cloning, tag it as high-priority synthetic inquiry.

4. Zero Black-Box

Every verdict published by TruthLens links to peer-reviewed sources and forensic hashes.
