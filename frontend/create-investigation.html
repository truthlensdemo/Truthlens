Formulating a Good Claim

TruthLens investigations evaluate specific factual assertions, not subjective value judgments, moral questions, or abstract sentiments.

Weak (Vague / Subjective)
"AI is bad for humanity and will ruin society."
Cannot be objectively falsified; expresses a subjective opinion rather than a testable proposition.
Better (Falsifiable Proposition)
"Generative AI models will reduce demand for entry-level software engineers by over 50% by 2027."
Defines specific variables: technology type, target workforce, quantitative measure, and timeframe.

Claim Formulation Rubric

  • Identifies specific actors: Names organizations, laws, entities, or distinct technologies.
  • Falsifiable by evidence: Can be corroborated or refuted using primary data and citations.
  • Timeframe or metric: Anchored to a date, measurable event, or quantitative claim.
What Happens Next?

Once created, you will enter the Evidence Workbench where you can attach primary records, assign credibility ratings, trace contradictions, and calculate evidentiary confidence.
