Key Synthesized Findings

3 Core Insights
1. Narrow Task Automation vs. Systems Architecture

Peer-reviewed evaluations (ACM) demonstrate that autonomous models excel at boilerplate syntax and isolated functions but degrade significantly in multi-threaded legacy codebases (introducing race conditions on 68% of refactoring tasks).

2. Macroeconomic Labor Demand Growth

Official labor statistics (U.S. BLS) forecast a 17% expansion in computer systems analyst and software development roles over the 2024–2034 horizon, directly contradicting the premise of industry-wide workforce obsolescence.

3. Human-in-the-Loop Requirement in Legal & Safety Standards

Emerging regulatory standards (IEEE, EU AI Act) mandate human review and architectural liability for enterprise code bases, making fully unassisted autonomous deployment commercially unfeasible.
