Credibility Rubric

TruthLens evaluates source authority using standard academic and open journalistic verification criteria.

5 / 5 Very High: Peer-reviewed scientific journals (ACM, Nature, IEEE), national statistical bodies (BLS, Census), verified court filings.
4 / 5 High: Major institutional news wires (Reuters, AP), accredited university press reports, official corporate 10-K SEC filings.
3 / 5 Moderate: Commercial industry whitepapers, trade publications, named on-the-record specialist interviews.
2 / 5 Low: Corporate keynote speeches, marketing blog posts, unverified think-tank op-eds with corporate funding.
1 / 5 Very Low: Anonymous social media screenshots, unsourced Telegram channels, speculative algorithmic aggregators.

Classification Guidance

  • Supporting: Empirically validates the target proposition under test.
  • Against: Presents falsifying datasets or directly contradicts the target proposition.
  • Neutral: Provides vital terminology, legal definitions, or historical context.
  • Needs Verification: Incomplete chain of custody or uncorroborated primary origin.
