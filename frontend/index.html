Taxonomy Of Truth

The Four Pillar Evidence Model

TruthLens avoids subjective labels like "Fake" or "Real." Every recorded piece of data is categorized by its precise evidentiary relationship to the claim.

Standards follow open journalistic audit protocols
Supporting Affirmative

Direct Corroboration

Documents, data series, empirical research, or primary audio/video recordings that substantiate the specific factual proposition.

Example: Official university registrar verification confirming the graduation date of a public official.
Against Disproving

Counter-Evidence

Verifiable proof, official denials with documentation, public records, or peer reviews that falsify or contradict the claim.

Example: Raw unedited video proving an alleged viral clip was deliberately cropped to reverse context.
Neutral Contextual

Contextual Background

Relevant reference materials, legal statutes, definitions, or historical context that inform the case without taking a side.

Example: Statutory definition of a legal classification referenced in an unverified news article.
Needs Verification Preliminary

Pending Provenance

Anonymous tips, unconfirmed social posts, or third-party claims currently undergoing chain-of-custody verification.

Example: An unindexed spreadsheet circulated on Telegram pending confirmation of its origin.
