1. Investigation Summary & Scope

This investigation was initiated to evaluate viral claims that autonomous generative code agents would eliminate software developers within 36 months. The investigation isolated the claim against empirical software engineering benchmarks (ACM/IEEE), official macroeconomic workforce datasets (U.S. BLS), and technical architectural constraints.

4. Final Evaluative Conclusion

Assigned Verdict: Mostly Unsupported
The claim that generative AI models will completely eliminate the demand for software engineering positions within three years is Mostly Unsupported by empirical research and contradicted by authoritative economic and employment data. While commercial keynote addresses demonstrated high success on isolated synthetic code snippets, peer-reviewed empirical studies demonstrate autonomous models introduce critical race conditions and architectural regressions when refactoring distributed legacy systems. Long-term employment projections from national labor bodies continue to forecast steady growth for the engineering profession.
